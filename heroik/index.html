HeroiK

Train, log, progress. A workout app that works at the gym, signal or no signal.

O que ele faz

Você monta suas rotinas e seus treinos, com os exercícios do catálogo ou com os seus. Durante o treino, registra cada série com carga e repetições, acompanha o descanso e vê o que fez da última vez, no mesmo exercício.

O treino inteiro funciona sem internet. O que você registrou sobe sozinho quando a conexão volta.

No fim, seu treino recebe uma nota e entra no histórico, com o volume, a evolução e a sequência da semana. O perfil guarda suas medalhas e os treinos que você escolheu mostrar.

E o que você faz pode ser compartilhado: publique um treino no feed, comente o de outra pessoa, entre num grupo e acompanhe o quadro de quem mais treinou.

What it does

You build your own routines and workouts, from the catalog or from exercises you create. While training, you log every set with load and reps, follow your rest, and see what you did last time on the same exercise.

The whole workout works without a connection. What you logged uploads on its own when the signal comes back.

At the end, your workout gets a grade and joins your history, with volume, progress and the week's streak. Your profile keeps your medals and the workouts you chose to show.

And what you do can be shared: post a workout to the feed, comment on someone else's, join a group and follow the board of who trained most.

Living together

HeroiK has a feed, comments and groups, so it has people — and therefore rules, and something to do when somebody breaks them. Any post or comment can be reported from the ⋯ menu, and whoever posted it can be blocked, both ways. A report for offensive content hides it from you immediately, and a person reads every report. Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.

What applies is in the Terms of Use; the step by step is in Support.
